## The business, in one paragraph (this is the only truth)

Rover makes ring travellers. Raw material is steel wire managed **by diameter**; each diameter has numbered **coils** sitting on **racks**. A **Rolling batch** takes a Traveller Type (which maps to exactly one diameter + F-thickness + F-width), a Traveller No, a Surface Finish, draws wire weight from one or more coils, and receives the next **Wire Serial** from the wire-serial master. From that moment the Wire Serial is the single identity of that material through **Rolling → Forming → Heat Treatment → Finishing → Finished Goods**. Every process has exactly two screens: a **Main Table** (material waiting to be initiated here + transactions in progress here) and a **Complete Table** (everything completed here). Completing a process hands its Output Weight to the next process, where it appears as "Finished Weight", auto-filled and never typed. Each process records only its own few completion values and its wastage (Finished − Output). There are no production orders, products, vendors, employees, shifts, QC inspections, stock transfers, adjustments, or quality-approval gates in this business. Master data is: Traveller Type, Traveller No, Surface Finish, Diameter (raw material), Coil, Rack, Diameter↔Traveller-Type mapping, Wire Serial, Machine.
