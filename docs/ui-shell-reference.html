Everything above this card is the reusable shell. Drop your own markup inside <main class="rover-content"> and leave the header and menu panel untouched.

The chevron at the top of the menu collapses it to a 72px icon rail. The choice is stored in localStorage["roverSidebarExpanded"] and re-applied by the inline script before first paint, so an expanded menu never flashes on a collapsed session.

Below 992px the menu becomes a drawer opened by the hamburger in the header, and the date block, search field and motto drop out via Bootstrap's d-* utilities.
