{% comment %} "Locate me": find a furnace load from anywhere in the app. One box. Type a batch number, a wire serial or a traveller type and the matches appear; a single match opens itself, and Enter takes the first one, so a hand scanner that types and presses Enter lands straight on the batch. Scanning the QR itself types its URL into the box - that is recognised and followed, rather than searched for. There is no camera here on purpose: a phone reads the printed label with its own camera app and opens the link. Rendered only for signed-in users; the JS lives in static/js/rover.js. {% endcomment %}
